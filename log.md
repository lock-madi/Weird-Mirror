## 9/21/2026 HW

### 1. Surveillance Simon Says - [Matthew Biederman](https://derivative.ca/community-post/commons-cameras-and-code-art-resistance-matthew-biederman/73246/)

An interactive installation where people stand on two sides of a box while cameras track their movements. A robotic voice gives commands, similar to Simon Says. The system tracks body movements and eye movements, measuring compliance and whether participants are perceived as a threat.

- **Inputs:** Cameras, body movements, eye movements, responses to commands
- **Outputs:** Robotic voice, tracking, compliance/threat assessments
- **Concept:** Makes participants think about surveillance and how people or algorithms monitor and judge them in everyday life.

### 2. One With the Universe - [Behance Project](https://www.behance.net/gallery/176072057/One-With-The-Universe-Interactive-Installation)

An interactive installation using body and hand tracking with projection mapping across three walls. A person in the center moves their hands toward and away from each other, causing spheres to float around and create ripples in the background. Another scene uses full-body tracking, turning the person's movements into pixels that fly into the air.

- **Inputs:** Hand tracking, full-body tracking, body movements
- **Outputs:** Floating spheres, ripples, pixels, projection-mapped visuals
- **Concept:** Physical movements transform into visual effects and responsive environments.

### 3. Interactive Light Character - [YouTube Short](https://youtube.com/shorts/F9ZRDTfJIaI?si=mBKsCXyIvhn27QMt)

An Xbox controller controls a small character made of light. The letter buttons change the character's size, while toggles control running speed and rotation. I'm not sure if it was made in TouchDesigner.

- **Inputs:** Xbox controller buttons and toggles
- **Outputs:** Changes in size, running speed, rotation, and character movement
- **Concept:** Using a game controller to manipulate an abstract visual character.

### 4. Interactive Guitar Visuals - [YouTube Short](https://youtube.com/shorts/KIDiBzUaJBs?si=7jbSdHLQ2LBVrrYJ)

A person plays guitar while the image behind them shifts and changes color and shape.

- **Inputs:** Guitar music/audio
- **Outputs:** Changes in color, shapes, and background visuals
- **Concept:** Music and performance creating responsive visuals.
